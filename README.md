### English

Free CNC software that runs in your browser, with no carve limit. Design shapes and text, V-carve and make inlays, carve
3D models (STL, OBJ, 3MF) and photos, cut and pocket DXF/SVG drawings or mill PCBs from Gerber files; simulate the job in
3D and stream it to your GRBL machine (Arduino CNC Shield, 3018 routers) over Web Serial. No installation — your files
never leave your computer.

- Design editor: shapes, text in 6 fonts, freehand, point editing, offset, union / subtract / intersect
- V-carving with flat-floor clearing, V-inlay plugs, textured pockets, raster pockets, ramped plunges
- 3D carving: roughing + ball-nose finishing, molds, cut the part free
- PCB: Gerber/Excellon, isolation, drilling, outline with tabs
- Nesting, fill the stock, tiling; apps: box maker, gear, maze, honeycomb, hole grid, radial array, dog-bones
- 3D simulation; jogging, Z zero / probe, auto-level, feed override, resume from line, machine profiles

[User guide](https://fixral3d.github.io/cnc-controller/en/guide.html)
